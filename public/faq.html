FAQ

Before you visit

Where can I visit a durian farm in Johor Bahru?

Durian City Farm is in Bandar Seri Alam, Johor Bahru, Johor, Malaysia. Visits are by appointment, so message us on WhatsApp first. You can taste our durians, walk around the farm and learn how durian is grown in one visit.

What durian varieties do you have?

Our signature variety is Black Thorn (D200). Musang King (D197) is not available yet. What’s available depends on the harvest.

Do you offer a durian buffet?

Not at the moment. Our durian buffet is currently unavailable. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll let you know when it returns.

When is durian season at Durian City Farm?

Our durian seasons are usually in December, March, June and September. Harvest timing can change with the weather, so message us on WhatsApp to check before you visit.

Can I buy durian to eat at the farm or take away?

Yes. We sell our durians for dine in at the farm or take away. Message us on WhatsApp to check what’s in season and arrange your order.

Does the farm tour include durian tasting?

Yes. Our Farm Tour is guided by our team and includes durian tasting at the farm.

Do you use organic fertilisers?

Yes. We use organic fertilisers on our durian trees to keep the soil healthy. You can see how we care for the trees on a Farm Tour.

Can visitors learn about durian cultivation?

Our Farm Learning visits are currently unavailable. You can still see how we grow our durians on a Farm Tour, guided by our team.

Can I hold an event at Durian City Farm?

Yes. We host corporate events, celebrations, private durian gatherings and school or community visits. Message us on WhatsApp with your date, number of guests and occasion, and we’ll plan it with you.

Can you bring durians to our office?

Yes. We can bring a durian party to your office or event venue. Message us on WhatsApp with your location, date and number of guests.

How early do I need to book?

Bookings must be made at least 24 hours in advance. Our time slots are 9:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 3:00 PM and 5:00 PM (last slot).

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Yes. All bookings require a deposit. After you send your booking request, we’ll share the deposit amount and payment details on WhatsApp, and your booking is confirmed once the deposit is received. For groups of more than 10 people, please WhatsApp us directly.

Can I stay overnight at the farm?

Our Durian City Farm Retreat is coming soon. Message us on WhatsApp to register your interest and we’ll let you know when bookings open.

How do I get to Durian City Farm?

The farm is in Bandar Seri Alam, Johor Bahru. Mobile signal can be limited nearby, so load your directions before you set off, or ask us for a location pin when you book.
